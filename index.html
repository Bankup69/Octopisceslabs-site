Why the octopus

Built to adapt

The octopus is one of nature's best examples of adaptation. It changes color and texture in a fraction of a second, solves problems, and fits through any gap larger than its beak.

Pair that with Pisces, the two fish, and you get the name. OctoPisces Labs is an independent studio in Nevada, run by an aviation maintenance tech who builds software with the same standard: check the work, then check it again.
