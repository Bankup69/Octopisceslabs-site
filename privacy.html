Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026

Who we are

We're OctoPisces Labs LLC, a small app studio in Nevada. This policy covers our website and every app we put out under the OctoPisces Labs name. Some apps handle data a little differently. When that happens, the app's store listing and its settings tell you.

What we collect

Only what an app needs to work. Here's what that can include.

  • What you type in. Workouts, budgets, property numbers or game progress.
  • Your account. If an app has sign-in, we keep your email address and a secure login.
  • Launch emails. Ask us to tell you when an app launches and we'll keep your email for that one announcement. Email us anytime to come off the list.
  • Crash reports. Basic crash and performance data, but only if you allow it on your device.

We don't touch your contacts, exact location, photos or microphone. The only exception is when an app clearly asks first and you say yes.

How we use it

We use your data to run the app and keep it synced across your devices. It also helps us find bugs and make the app better.

If you email support, we use what you send to answer you.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data. Ever. We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers either.

Companies that help us

Some apps rely on outside services to run. Supabase stores data and handles sign-in. Apple and Google deliver the apps and handle in-app payments. RevenueCat and Stripe handle anything you buy on our website. They only use your data to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. Want it gone? Email ellisbyo@octopisceslabs.com and we'll delete your account and everything tied to it. It's done within 30 days.

Kids

Our apps aren't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child gave us data, tell us and we'll delete it.

If this policy changes

We'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also show up in the apps they affect.

Contact

Privacy questions go to ellisbyo@octopisceslabs.com.
