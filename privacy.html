Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Who we are

OctoPisces Labs LLC is an independent app studio based in Nevada. This policy covers our website and every app we publish under the OctoPisces Labs name. If an app handles data differently from what is described here, that app's store listing and in-app settings say so.

What we collect

We collect only what an app needs to work.

  • Data you enter. Workouts, budgets, property figures or game progress you put into an app.
  • Account details. If an app offers sign-in, your email address and a securely stored login.
  • Basic diagnostics. Crash reports and performance data, when you allow it on your device.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos or microphone audio unless an app clearly asks for it and you grant permission.

How we use it

  • To run the app and sync your data between your devices.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

Some apps store data with trusted providers that run our backend, such as Supabase for databases and sign-in, and Apple or Google for app distribution and payments. These providers process data only to deliver their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing ellisbyo@octopisceslabs.com. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the affected apps.

Contact

Questions about privacy: ellisbyo@octopisceslabs.com
