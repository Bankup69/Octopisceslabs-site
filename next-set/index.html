What it does

Every workout adapts the next

Your reps, weight and effort move your training maxes. Hard or cut-short runs shrink the next ones; fast ones speed up your paces.

Programs built for you

Strength, muscle, running or HIIT, or two blended. 4 to 24 weeks, 2 to 6 days a week, at your level.

Runs from your watch

Runs come in through Apple Health or Health Connect, including best efforts from your watch.

Swap anything

Sore knee? Swap the run for the bike, rower, elliptical, stairs or pool. Machine taken? Swap the exercise. The plan keeps adapting.

Challenges

Swim Alcatraz, climb Kilimanjaro, ride a Grand Tour, race a triathlon, or get ready for a military fitness test, with a plan that adapts.

Medals worth earning

Struck-metal medals for plate milestones, summits and test passes. Your best effort is gold. Share any medal as an image.

Form help

Three cues and the most common mistake for every exercise, plus a link to demo videos.

Train with friends

Team challenges add everyone's miles into one total, and opt-in leaderboards rank the fastest.
