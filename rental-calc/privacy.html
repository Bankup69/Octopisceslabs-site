Privacy Policy

Rental Calc (the "app") is a calculator for evaluating rental property deals, made by OctoPisces Labs LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. The properties and numbers you enter are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and purchase status is checked through our payments provider, RevenueCat, using an anonymous ID. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Links you add

If you add a listing link (for example from Zillow or Redfin), it is stored with the property on your device. The app reads the address from the link itself and does not contact the listing site.
