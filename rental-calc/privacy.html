Privacy Policy

EquityFoundry (the "app") helps you figure out if a rental property is a good deal. It's made by OctoPisces Labs LLC.

Your property data

The properties and numbers you enter in the calculator stay on your device. We don't upload them. The app shows no ads.

Usage counts

So we know whether the app is useful, we count a few things: a visit to the calculator, the first time you change a number, opening the Pro screen, opening Ingot, and the first property or entry you add in Ingot. Each count is saved with our database provider, Supabase. If you came from one of our ads, posts or example links, we also note which one (for example "meta" and "maple-loss"), or the website that linked you. We don't save your name, email, account, property numbers or any cookie with these counts, and we don't use them to track you across other sites.

Ingot (portfolio tracker)

What you log in Ingot (your properties, rent and expense entries, distributions, maintenance log and import rules) is saved on your device first. If you're signed in, it's also saved to your account with Supabase so it stays the same on all your devices. Only you can read it: the database lets each account see its own data and nothing else.

Bank files you import are read on your device. We store the entries you choose to import, not the file. Receipt and repair photos stay on the device that took them.

You can download all of it any time (Ingot → Taxes → Download everything). Deleting your account deletes the synced copy.

Accounts

You don't need an account. You only need one to buy Pro on the website, or to use Pro on more than one device.

If you make one, we store your email address and a random account ID with our sign-in provider, Supabase. That's how we email you sign-in codes and recognize your purchases.

If you set a password, Supabase stores it scrambled (hashed), so nobody, including us, can read it. If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture so we can create your account. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

We also store what you add on the Account page: your name, your email preferences, and your app settings (like appearance and the starting numbers for new properties). Your saved properties stay on your device.

Want your account deleted? Use Delete account on the Account page, or email us (below) and we'll do it within 30 days.

Launch announcement list

Tap "Notify me" and we'll store your email so we can tell you when the iPhone and Android apps launch. That's the only thing we use it for. We never sell it or share it. If you're signed in, you can turn it off on the Account page. Anyone can email us to come off the list.

Other emails

Product updates and investing tips are off unless you turn them on under Email preferences on the Account page. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.

Purchases

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles Pro purchases in the apps. On the website, Stripe handles them through our payments provider, RevenueCat.

Your purchase status is linked to your account ID. For website purchases, RevenueCat and Stripe also get your email address so they can send the receipt. We never see your card details. You can read RevenueCat's and Stripe's privacy policies.

Links you add

If you add a listing link, like one from Zillow or Redfin, it's saved with that property on your device. The app reads the address straight from the link. It never contacts the listing site.
