Privacy Policy

Rental Calc (the "app") is a calculator for evaluating rental property deals, made by OctoPisces Labs LLC.

Your property data

The properties and numbers you enter are stored only on your device. We don’t upload them, and the app has no analytics and no advertising.

Accounts

An account is optional. You need one only to buy Pro on the website, or to use a Pro purchase on more than one device. If you create one, we store your email address and a random account ID with our sign-in provider, Supabase, so we can email you sign-in codes and recognize your purchases. You can delete your account by emailing us (below); we do this within 30 days.

Launch announcement list

If you tap “Notify me”, we store your email address so we can tell you when the iPhone and Android apps launch. We use it only for that announcement and never sell or share it. Email us to be removed at any time.

Purchases

Pro purchases in the apps are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Purchases on the website are handled by Stripe through our payments provider, RevenueCat. Your purchase status is linked to your account ID, and for website purchases RevenueCat and Stripe also receive your email address for the receipt. We never see your card details. See RevenueCat’s and Stripe’s privacy policies.

Links you add

If you add a listing link (for example from Zillow or Redfin), it is stored with the property on your device. The app reads the address from the link itself and does not contact the listing site.
