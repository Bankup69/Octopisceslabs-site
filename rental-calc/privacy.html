Privacy Policy

Rental Calc (the "app") helps you figure out if a rental property is a good deal. It's made by OctoPisces Labs LLC.

Your property data

The properties and numbers you enter stay on your device. We don't upload them. The app has no analytics and no ads.

Accounts

You don't need an account. You only need one to buy Pro on the website, or to use Pro on more than one device.

If you make one, we store your email address and a random account ID with our sign-in provider, Supabase. That's how we email you sign-in codes and recognize your purchases. Want your account deleted? Email us (below) and we'll do it within 30 days.

Launch announcement list

Tap "Notify me" and we'll store your email so we can tell you when the iPhone and Android apps launch. That's the only thing we use it for. We never sell it or share it. Email us anytime to come off the list.

Purchases

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles Pro purchases in the apps. On the website, Stripe handles them through our payments provider, RevenueCat.

Your purchase status is linked to your account ID. For website purchases, RevenueCat and Stripe also get your email address so they can send the receipt. We never see your card details. You can read RevenueCat's and Stripe's privacy policies.

Links you add

If you add a listing link, like one from Zillow or Redfin, it's saved with that property on your device. The app reads the address straight from the link. It never contacts the listing site.
