Delete your Next Set account

You can delete your account and all of its data whenever you want.

In the app

  1. Open Next Set and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

That's it. Your account, profile, programs, workout logs and support usage records are wiped from our live systems right away. Encrypted backups expire within 7 days.

Can't get into the app?

Email privacy@octopisceslabs.com from the address on your account. Use the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm with you and delete it within 30 days.

Heads up. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel that in your store settings first.
