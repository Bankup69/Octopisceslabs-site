Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal information OctoPisces Labs LLC ("we") collects through the Next Set app and related services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (or an Apple private relay address) and an account identifier from that provider.

Training information you enter. Programs you choose, workout logs (exercises, weights, reps, effort ratings, run distances, times and paces, interval rounds), notes, and optional profile details such as name, bodyweight, resting and maximum heart rate, lift maxes and race times.

Check-in information. Optional daily ratings of sleep, energy and soreness. This information relates to your health and fitness. We use it only to show it back to you and, in the future, to adjust your training; we do not use it for advertising.

Apple Health and Health Connect (optional). If you turn on the Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) connection in Settings, the app reads, with your permission: workouts and their distance and duration, sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability. This data is read on your phone to suggest values for your workout log (for example a run's distance and time, or last night's sleep) and to show a recovery suggestion. Raw Health data is not uploaded to our servers. Values you choose to keep in a workout log are saved with that log like anything else you enter. We never write to Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, sold, shared with third parties, or used to train AI models. You can turn the connection off in Settings at any time, and change permissions in the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).

Team challenges (optional). If you create or join a team, the display name you choose and your progress on that challenge (for example, miles swum) are visible to the other members of that team. Nothing else about you or your training is shared with them. Leave a team at any time to stop sharing; deleting your account removes you from every team.

Leaderboards (optional, off by default). If you turn on leaderboards, the display name you choose and your best results (fastest run times at standard distances, and how many times and how fast you've completed challenges) are visible to everyone using the app. Turn it off to remove them; deleting your account removes them too.

Reports about names. If you report a display name or team name, we store who reported it, whose name it was, the name, and when, so we can review it. Names that break our rules, or that several people report, are replaced with a neutral name. Hiding a person only affects your own phone and isn't sent to us.

Exports. If you export your workouts, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Support conversations. Messages you send to the AI support assistant, together with a short summary of your current program, are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store the content of these conversations; we keep only a daily count of messages to prevent abuse. If you email support, we keep that correspondence.

Consent records. The date and version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and your confirmation that you meet the minimum age.

Technical information. Our hosting and authentication providers process technical data such as IP address, device and app version, and request logs to operate and secure the service. If the app hits an error, it sends us the error message, the screen you were on, the app version and your phone's platform (iPhone or Android), linked to your account, so we can fix it. Error reports don't include your workouts.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos, and we do not upload data from Apple Health or Health Connect except values you choose to save in a workout log. We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: create your account, store and sync your programs and workouts, and calculate your next workouts.
  • To provide support, including the AI assistant.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security and changes to our terms.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

We use service providers that process data on our behalf under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Supabase – database, authentication and server hosting.
  • Anthropic – generates replies for the AI support assistant from the messages you send to it. [CONFIRM ANTHROPIC'S CURRENT DATA RETENTION AND USE TERMS FOR YOUR ACCOUNT BEFORE PUBLISHING.]
  • Apple and Google – app distribution, sign-in if you choose it, and payments if you subscribe.
  • RevenueCat – checks whether your subscription is active. It receives your account ID (a random identifier, not your name or email) and your App Store or Google Play purchase records. We don't receive your payment card details.
  • Resend – sends account emails such as confirmations and password resets.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or as part of a merger or acquisition (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

How long we keep it

We keep your account and training information until you delete your account. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your account, profile, programs, workout logs and support usage records from our active systems immediately. Copies in encrypted backups are removed as backups expire, within 7 days. We keep support emails for up to 24 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings, Your data, Copy backup gives you a copy of your training data.
  • Correction: edit any workout, profile or setting in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings, Account, Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, including to object to or restrict processing, data portability, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact privacy@octopisceslabs.com to exercise them. We will not discriminate against you for doing so.

Legal bases (EEA/UK users). We process account and training data to perform our contract with you; health-related check-in data with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by not entering it and deleting past entries; and security and abuse-prevention data based on our legitimate interests. [CONFIRM WITH YOUR LAWYER IF YOU LAUNCH IN THE EEA OR UK, INCLUDING INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS.]

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data in our database is restricted to your signed-in account by row-level security rules. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact privacy@octopisceslabs.com and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If changes are material, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
